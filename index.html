01 / The Lab

A place to explore what comes next.

01

The Applied AI Lab brings together students, faculty, and external partners to investigate emerging technology, develop new ideas, and turn complex questions into working solutions.

It is designed for both technical depth and open exploration. Members can learn new tools, collaborate across disciplines, prototype ideas, test systems, and contribute to projects with academic or real-world impact.

The goal is not simply to study artificial intelligence. It is to understand it by building with it.

02 / Direction

Mission & vision.

Advanced technology becomes more useful when people can question it, experiment with it, and apply it across different fields.

Our Mission

Provide methods for students to get involved with groundbreaking technology and teach them how to apply these tools into their field of study.

Our Vision

Create a showcase for the Applied AI Lab that encourages students from various interdisciplinary studies to use the tools offered to showcase their own projects, coordinate projects or events, learn more about AI tools and news, and support other students as fellows when it comes to working with AI.
