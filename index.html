Our Mission

Where Analytics, AI & Ideas Come Together.

The Quinnipiac Analytics & AI Lab connects students, faculty, and industry through hands-on exploration of data, analytics, and artificial intelligence. Our mission is to create opportunities for collaboration, experimentation, and learning that turn ideas into meaningful real-world impact.

About the Lab

A place to explore what comes next.

01

The Analytics & AI Lab is a collaborative space for the Quinnipiac community to explore how analytics and artificial intelligence can be applied across disciplines.

The Lab brings together students, faculty, and industry partners to learn new technologies, develop projects, exchange ideas, and solve real-world problems. Whether someone has years of technical experience or is exploring analytics and AI for the first time, the Lab provides opportunities to learn, contribute, and collaborate.

The goal isn't simply to study emerging technology. It's to use it, experiment with it, and build with it.

Purpose & Vision

Building a community around analytics & AI.

P

Our Purpose

Make analytics and AI more accessible across Quinnipiac by connecting people with the knowledge, technology, resources, and opportunities needed to explore them.

V

Our Vision

Build an interdisciplinary community where analytics and AI bring together different schools, fields, and perspectives—and where ideas can develop into projects with meaningful academic and real-world applications.
